The open strings spell D#m(add11): D#, F#, G# and A#, four of the five notes of D# minor pentatonic. The missing one, C#, sits at fret 3 on string 5 and fret 5 on string 2.

Pink rings above the nut are open strings. Most chords here leave strings 2 and 1 open, so G#3 and D#4 ring under nearly everything as a drone. Teal marks the root.

Strings 5 and 4 go up a half step, about 12% more tension. String 2 drops three half steps and loses about 29%, so it may flap or buzz; a heavier string 2 helps.

Barre strings 6 to 4 and add one finger on string 3: the same fret gives a minor chord, one fret higher gives a major one. Strings 2 and 1 stay open, and their G# and D# fit every stop. Frets are drawn to real spacing.
