How do I play…

Lowercase b = flat (Bb, addb6, m7b5); capital B = the note B (Si), e.g. C/B. Spanish names work too: Sib, Fa#m7, Re/Fa#. Searches the current tuning; tap a shape to put it on the neck and hear it.

Playback

Tap a fret to place a note; tap it again to mute the string. The top row is the open string.

Instrument and tuning

Low string first. Octave numbers are optional: without one, each note lands closest to the string's current pitch.
