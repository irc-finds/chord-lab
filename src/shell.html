How do I play…

Lowercase b = flat (Bb, addb6, m7b5); capital B = the note B (Si), e.g. C/B. Spanish names work too: Sib, Fa#m7, Re/Fa#. Searches the current tuning; tap a shape to put it on the neck and hear it.

Sound and pedals

Tap a pedal to switch it on and show its knobs, then tap ▶ to hear it. Use ↑ ↓ to move a pedal earlier or later in the chain; the numbers show the order the sound goes through. Picking a rig also puts the pedals back in the standard order.

Playback

Tap a fret to place a note; tap it again to mute the string. The top row is the open string.
